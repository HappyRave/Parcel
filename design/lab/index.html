Parcel lab R1 · main window

Four different ideas for what Parcel opens to. The mechanics come from GlassWire (a live graph, then who is talking to whom); the look is our own. Same mock data in every variant. Logos are placeholder monograms: the real ones are each site's favicon, fetched directly from the site (decided). Countries are shown as postage-stamp ISO codes, because Windows doesn't render flag emoji.

A

Timeline

CLAUDE'S PICK

Idea: one mirrored graph across the top (download above the line, upload below), with apps on the left and the hosts the selected app talks to on the right. Everything answers "what's happening now, and who". Why the pick: it reads in two seconds, it scales from a quiet PC to a 4K stream, and the map (B) and flow (C) views can become tabs later without changing this layout.

B

Atlas

Idea: geography first. A dotted world map with an arc from you to every remote host; parcels (dots) travel the arcs as traffic flows, and thicker arcs carry more bytes. Countries are ranked on the right; a thin live graph runs along the bottom. Strong: the most "wow", and instantly shows where data goes. Weak: apps are secondary, and the map is crowded by large CDNs that all sit in the same few cities. The map outline here is a rough placeholder.

C

Flow

Idea: apps on the left pour into companies on the right; ribbon width is bytes in the selected range. Selecting a ribbon lists its hosts. Strong: answers "which app talks to which company" in one picture, including surprises (a game talking to an ad network). Weak: poor for "right now"; it needs a time range to be meaningful.

D

Manifest

Idea: a compact live shipping log. One row per connection, newest first, each with a tracking number; active connections on top, recently closed ones fading below. No graph, a small window. Strong: densest and the most "Parcel"; great as a mini mode next to work. Weak: no overview of volume over time; long sessions become a wall of rows.

Light palette

Same home crop, three light palettes. Dark stays as it is.

L1Paper CLAUDE'S PICK

Warm off-white, the dark theme's warm ink inverted. Calm, and the kraft accent still reads.

L2Frost

Cool neutral grey like Windows 11's own apps. Blends in; loses the warmth.

L3Kraft

Tinted like packing paper. The most "Parcel"; a tinted background makes app colours a little muddier.

History tab

Looking back over the 30 days of hourly data and the daily data kept indefinitely.

Y1Timeline + range CLAUDE'S PICK

Daily bars by app in the same mirrored language as Live; drag to select a range, and the tables below follow it.

Y2Calendar

A month grid shaded by volume; click a day for its graph and top lists. Good for "which day", weak for trends.

Y3Daily receipts

One card per day: totals, top apps, countries. Very readable, but hard to compare across weeks.
